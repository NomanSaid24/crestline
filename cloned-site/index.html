About Us

Founded in 1982, Crestline (SMC-PVT) Limited is a trusted manufacturer and exporter of custom promotional textiles, specializing in premium cotton and poly/cotton products for the global promotional industry.

Our Vision

To be the leading provider of responsible, high-quality promotional textiles for global brands. Crestline combines premium materials, compliance-focused manufacturing, and dependable service to support long-term customer partnerships across global markets.

QUALITY - CONSISTENCY - SUSTAINABILITY

Our Mission

Our mission is to deliver quality, responsibly made textile products at scale while maintaining ethical standards and dependable production timelines. We work closely with customers to develop tailored solutions that align with brand requirements, product specifications, and market needs.
